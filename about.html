ABOUT.

FRANK OCEAN IS A SINGER, SONGWRITER, RECORD PRODUCER, AND ARTIST KNOWN FOR HIS INTROSPECTIVE LYRICS, EXPERIMENTAL R&B, AND MINIMALIST APPROACH TO MUSIC. HE WAS BORN IN 1987 AND HE GREW UP MOVING AROUND A LOT BEFORE HE SETTLED IN NEW ORLEANS. HE ACTUALLY WANTED TO BE A PRODUCER FIRST BEFORE HE EVEN THOUGHT ABOUT SINGING HIS OWN SONGS. HE WROTE FOR OTHER ARTISTS FOR YEARS BEFORE ANYONE EVEN KNEW HIS NAME.

HE STARTED OUT WITH A GROUP CALLED ODD FUTURE EVEN THOUGH HE ISNT REALLY A RAPPER LIKE THE REST OF THEM. HE JUST FIT IN BECAUSE THEY WERE ALL WEIRD AND DIDNT CARE WHAT ANYBODY THOUGHT OF THEM. THATS PROBALY WHY HIS MUSIC SOUNDS SO DIFFERENT FROM EVERYTHING ELSE. HE DOESNT FOLLOW NO FORMULA HE JUST MAKES WHATEVER FEELS RIGHT TO HIM EVEN IF IT DOESNT SOUND LIKE A NORMAL SONG.

IN 2011 HE RELEASED A FREE MIXTAPE CALLED NOSTALGIA ULTRA AND EVERYBODY WENT CRAZY FOR IT BECAUSE NO ONE HAD HEARD ANYTHING LIKE IT BEFORE. THEN IN 2012 HE WROTE A LETTER ON HIS TUMBLR TALKING ABOUT HIS FIRST LOVE BEING A MAN AND THAT WAS A REALLY BIG DEAL BACK THEN ESPECIALLY IN HIP HOP WHERE NOBODY TALKED LIKE THAT. IT TOOK ALOT OF COURAGE AND I THINK THATS WHY SO MANY PEOPLE LOOK UP TO HIM NOT JUST FOR THE MUSIC BUT FOR BEING HIMSELF NO MATTER WHAT.

AFTER THAT HE KEPT GETTING MORE PRIVATE. HE DOESNT DO ALOT OF INTERVIEWS AND HE DOESNT POST MUCH. SOME PEOPLE GET MAD BECAUSE HE TAKES SO LONG BETWEEN ALBUMS BUT I THINK THATS PART OF WHY HE FEELS SO SPECIAL. HE DOESNT MAKE MUSIC JUST TO STAY RELEVANT HE ONLY DROPS SOMETHING WHEN ITS ACTUALLY READY. HE ALSO HAS A RADIO SHOW CALLED BLONDED RADIO WHICH IS WHERE THE TITLE OF THIS WEBSITE COMES FROM.

HIS VOICE SOUNDS LIKE ITS FLOATING SOMETIMES. LIKE HES SINGING FROM SOMEWHERE FAR AWAY BUT IT STILL FEELS CLOSE TO YOU. I DONT KNOW HOW TO EXPLAIN IT BUT IF U LISTEN TO CHANNEL ORANGE OR BLONDE AT NIGHT WITH HEADPHONES ON YOU WILL UNDERSTAND WHAT I MEAN. HE JUST GETS IT. HE GETS WHAT ITS LIKE TO MISS SOMEONE OR TO GROW UP AND NOT KNOW WHO U ARE ANYMORE.

ANYWAYS THATS FRANK FOR U.
