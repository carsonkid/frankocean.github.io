NOSTALGIA, ULTRA.

2011. FREE MIXTAPE.

ITS BASICLY A BOY DRIVING AROUND AT NIGHT THINKING ABOUT A GIRL HE CANT GET OVER. EVERYTHING FEELS LIKE A MEMORY EVEN THE NEW STUFF. HE USES OLD SONGS AND BENDS THEM INTO SOMETHING SADDER. THIS IS WHERE HIS WHOLE SOUND STARTED.

CHANNEL ORANGE.

2012.

A BUNCH OF LITTLE STORYS ABOUT RICH KIDS IN LA WHO ARE BORED AND LONELY EVEN THOUGH THEY HAVE EVERYTHING. THERES A SONG ABOUT LOVING SOMEONE WHO DOESNT LOVE U BACK THE SAME WAY AND ANOTHER ABOUT A GUY WHOS ADDICTED TO DRUGS. IT JUMPS AROUND LIKE UR FLIPPING THROUGH TV CHANNELS THATS WHY ITS CALLED THAT.

BLONDE.

2016.

THIS ONE IS ABOUT GROWING UP AND REALIZING NOTHING STAYS THE SAME. HE TALKS ABOUT HIS MOM, HIS FIRST CAR, LOSING FRIENDS, AND A LOVE THAT NEVER REALLY WORKED OUT. ITS QUIETER THAN HIS OTHER ALBUMS LIKE HES WHISPERING THE WHOLE THING TO HIMSELF. MOST PEOPLE SAY THIS IS HIS BEST ONE.

ENDLESS.

2016. VISUAL ALBUM.

HE DROPPED THIS RIGHT BEFORE BLONDE WHILE A VIDEO PLAYED OF HIM BUILDING A STAIRCASE OVER AND OVER. ITS ABOUT WAITING AND BUILDING SOMETHING WITH UR OWN HANDS EVEN IF IT FEELS LIKE IT NEVER ENDS. VERY UNDERATED IN MY OPINION.
